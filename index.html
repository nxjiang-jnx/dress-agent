Problem Setting

Simulation-ready garment reconstruction from a single image

Given a single in-the-wild image, we recover every garment of the outfit as a sewing pattern. The patterns are simulation-ready, so they drape and animate on the body, and directly serve 3D and 4D generation.

Interactive Viewer

Drag to turn the outfit in 3D. Click a layer below to peel the outfit down to it, and the sewing pattern on the left follows.
